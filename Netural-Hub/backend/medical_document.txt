Hypertension Management Guidelines:
Blood pressure target for adults is below 130/80 mmHg.
First-line treatment includes ACE inhibitors like lisinopril or ARBs like losartan.
Thiazide diuretics are recommended for older patients.
Beta blockers are used when heart failure or arrhythmia is present.
Calcium channel blockers like amlodipine are effective for isolated systolic hypertension.
Lifestyle changes include low sodium diet less than 2300mg per day.
Regular aerobic exercise 30 minutes daily reduces blood pressure by 5-8 mmHg.
DASH diet is highly recommended for hypertensive patients.
Monitor kidney function and electrolytes regularly when on ACE inhibitors.

Cardiac Arrhythmia Guidelines:
Early signs include palpitations, dizziness, chest pain, and shortness of breath.
ECG is the primary diagnostic tool for arrhythmia.
Atrial fibrillation is the most common sustained arrhythmia.
Beta blockers and calcium channel blockers are used for rate control.
Anticoagulation with warfarin or DOACs reduces stroke risk in atrial fibrillation.
Cardioversion may be required for unstable patients.
Implantable cardioverter defibrillator is recommended for high risk patients.

Mental Health and Therapy Guidelines:
Cognitive Behavioral Therapy is first line treatment for depression and anxiety.
SSRIs like fluoxetine and sertraline are first line pharmacotherapy for depression.
Therapy sessions should be scheduled weekly for acute cases.
Sleep hygiene is critical for mental health recovery.
Mindfulness and meditation reduce anxiety symptoms by 40 percent.
Exercise releases endorphins and reduces depression symptoms significantly.
Social support networks improve therapy outcomes by 60 percent.

Medication Interactions:
Metformin should not be combined with contrast dye without 48 hour washout.
Warfarin interacts with NSAIDs increasing bleeding risk significantly.
ACE inhibitors combined with potassium sparing diuretics can cause hyperkalemia.
Statins combined with fibrates increase risk of rhabdomyolysis.
Aspirin and clopidogrel combination increases gastrointestinal bleeding risk.
Avoid grapefruit juice with statins and calcium channel blockers.
